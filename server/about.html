About Samarth Enterprises

Your Trusted Partner Since 2024

Our Story

Established in 2024, Samarth Enterprises has grown into a trusted provider of manpower, labour, security, housekeeping, and gardening services. We began with the goal of offering dependable workforce solutions for homes, residential societies, commercial buildings, and industries. Over time, our dedication to quality and professionalism helped us expand into a multi-service organisation that people rely on every day.

Whether it's a trained security guard for a commercial site, a housekeeper for daily cleaning, or gardening support to maintain green spaces — we provide skilled manpower that delivers consistency, safety, and care.

We believe in transparent service, responsible workers, and long-term relationships built on trust.

Our Mission

To deliver reliable, trained manpower that enhances safety, cleanliness, and efficiency for homes and workplaces. We are committed to professionalism, discipline, and customer satisfaction in every service we provide.

Our Vision

To become one of the most trusted and preferred manpower & security service providers in the region. We aim to uplift our workforce through better training, skill development, and fair opportunities while ensuring top-quality service for every client.
